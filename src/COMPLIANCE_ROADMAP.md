## ❌ CRITICAL GAPS

### 1. **Gas Safety Compliance** (CRITICAL)
**Missing**: Centralized Gas Safety (CP12) register with:
- Annual renewal tracking
- Tenant copy service records
- Engineer Gas Safe registration verification
- Property-specific certificate tracking
- Automatic reminders at 10 months, 11 months, 12 months

**Legislation**: Gas Safety (Installation and Use) Regulations 1998

### 2. **EICR Compliance** (CRITICAL)
**Missing**: Dedicated EICR management:
- 5-year cycle tracking
- Remedial work deadlines (28 days for C1/C2 codes)
- Electrical contractor qualifications
- Distribution to tenants within 28 days

**Legislation**: Electrical Safety Standards in the Private Rented Sector (England) Regulations 2020

### 3. **Deposit Protection** (CRITICAL)
**Missing**: Tenancy deposit tracking:
- 30-day protection deadline
- Prescribed information service
- Scheme reference numbers
- End-of-tenancy deduction management
- Dispute tracking

**Legislation**: Housing Act 2004 s.212-215

### 4. **Right to Rent** (CRITICAL)
**Missing**: Systematic Right to Rent checks:
- Initial check before tenancy
- Follow-up checks (12 months after, or before expiry)
- Document verification records
- Home Office reporting workflow

**Legislation**: Immigration Act 2014, Immigration Act 2016

### 5. **How to Rent Guide** (CRITICAL)
**Missing**: Service tracking:
- Latest version verification
- Service date records
- Digital + physical delivery confirmation
- Version control

**Legislation**: Assured Shorthold Tenancy Notices and Prescribed Requirements (England) Regulations 2015

### 6. **EPC Compliance** (HIGH)
**Missing**: MEES enforcement:
- Minimum band E requirement
- Exemption registration
- 10-year validity tracking
- Proposed 2030 band C upgrade path

**Legislation**: Minimum Energy Efficiency Standards (MEES) Regulations 2015

### 7. **Smoke & CO Alarms** (HIGH)
**Missing**: Testing compliance:
- Installation records (every floor, every room with solid fuel)
- Test on day 1 of tenancy
- Annual testing during periodic inspections
- Battery replacement logs

**Legislation**: Smoke and Carbon Monoxide Alarm (England) Regulations 2015

### 8. **Legionella Risk Assessment** (HIGH)
**Missing**: Water safety compliance:
- Assessment frequency (every 2 years recommended)
- Control measures implementation
- Temperature monitoring records
- Landlord responsibilities documentation

**Legislation**: Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974, COSHH Regulations 2002

### 9. **Fire Safety Act 2021** (HIGH)
**Missing**: Enhanced fire safety:
- External wall system assessments
- Flat entrance door inspections
- Fire door maintenance (annual checks)
- Personal Emergency Evacuation Plans (PEEPs) for vulnerable residents

**Legislation**: Fire Safety Act 2021, Fire Safety (England) Regulations 2022

### 10. **HMO Licensing** (HIGH)
**Missing**: HMO compliance:
- Mandatory licensing (5+ people, 2+ households)
- Additional licensing schemes
- Selective licensing areas
- Licence renewal tracking
- Property-specific conditions

**Legislation**: Housing Act 2004 Part 2

### 11. **Tenant Fees Act 2019** (MEDIUM)
**Missing**: Fee compliance:
- Permitted payments tracker
- Prohibited payment detection
- Holding deposit caps (1 week)
- Security deposit caps (5 weeks)
- Default fee calculations

**Legislation**: Tenant Fees Act 2019

### 12. **Data Protection (GDPR)** (MEDIUM)
**Missing**: Data compliance:
- Tenant data processing records
- Right to erasure workflow
- Data retention schedules
- Privacy notice service
- Subject access request management

**Legislation**: UK GDPR, Data Protection Act 2018

### 13. **Anti-Social Behaviour** (MEDIUM)
**Missing**: ASB case management:
- Incident logging
- Warning letters
- Injunction tracking
- Possession ground documentation
- Multi-agency working records

**Legislation**: Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014

### 14. **Decent Homes Standard** (FUTURE)
**Missing**: Preparation for PRS extension:
- Property condition assessments
- Hazard category tracking (HHSRS)
- Improvement planning
- Compliance gap analysis

**Legislation**: Expected 2035-37 extension to PRS

### 15. **Awaab's Law** (FUTURE)
**Missing**: Damp & mould compliance:
- Hazard reporting within 24 hours
- Investigation within 7 days
- Remediation within 15 days (urgent) / 28 days (non-urgent)
- Resident communication logs

**Legislation**: Social Housing (Regulation) Act 2023, expected PRS 2035-37

```
Compliance Score = (Compliant Requirements / Total Requirements) × 100

Weighted by severity:
- Critical (gas safety, deposits): 3× weight
- High (EICR, HMO): 2× weight
- Medium (EPC, fees): 1× weight
```
