## Common Questions

### Q: I lost my portal link. What do I do?
**A:** Contact your property manager - they can generate a new link instantly.

### Q: Can I access on my phone?
**A:** Yes! The portal works perfectly on mobile devices.

### Q: Is it safe to pay rent online?
**A:** Absolutely. Payments use bank-level encryption via Stripe.

### Q: How do I set up automatic payments?
**A:** Go to Payments tab → Click "Manage" on Auto-Pay → Enter card details → Choose payment day.

### Q: Can I upload photos for maintenance?
**A:** Yes! Photos help us understand the issue better.

### Q: How do I contact my property manager?
**A:** Use the Messages tab - it's the fastest way to reach us.

### Q: Where can I find my tenancy agreement?
**A:** Check the Docs tab - all your documents are there.

### Q: Will I get a receipt for payment?
**A:** Yes! Receipts are automatically generated and available in Documents.

## Payment Options

### One-Time Payment
```
1. Click "Pay Now"
2. Enter card details
3. Confirm amount
4. Instant confirmation
5. Receipt generated
```

### Automatic Monthly Payments
```
1. Click "Manage" on Auto-Pay
2. Choose payment day (e.g., 1st of month)
3. Enter card details once
4. Automatic charge every month
5. Email confirmation each time
6. Cancel anytime
```

## Maintenance Request Flow

```
1. Click "New Request"
   ↓
2. Fill in details
   - Title (e.g., "Leaking tap")
   - Description
   - Category (plumbing, electrical, etc.)
   - Priority (emergency, urgent, standard)
   ↓
3. Upload photos (optional but helpful)
   ↓
4. Submit
   ↓
5. Track progress visually
   Reported → Assigned → In Progress → Completed
   ↓
6. Receive updates via notifications
```

## Status Indicators

### Payment Status
- 🟢 **Paid** - Payment received
- 🟡 **Pending** - Payment processing
- 🔴 **Overdue** - Payment late - contact us
- 🟠 **Partial** - Part payment received

### Maintenance Status
- ⚪ **Reported** - Request received
- 🔵 **Assigned** - Contractor allocated
- 🟣 **In Progress** - Work being done
- 🟢 **Completed** - Issue resolved

### Tenancy Status
- 🟢 **Active** - Current tenancy
- 🟡 **In Arrears** - Rent overdue
- 🟠 **Notice Given** - Moving out
- ⚪ **Former** - Previous tenant
