# Beta User Testimonial Video — Interview Script

**Duration:** 2-3 minutes | **Format:** One-on-one interview | **Goal:** Social proof + credibility

---

## PRE-INTERVIEW BRIEFING (Send to Beta User)

> Hi [Name],
> 
> We'd love to feature you in a short testimonial video about your experience with Premiso. It's just a casual 10-minute conversation—no acting required!
>
> **What we'll ask:**
> - Your role and how many properties you manage
> - The pain point you had before Premiso
> - One specific thing that changed after switching
> - Whether you'd recommend it (and why)
>
> **Timeline:** [Date/Time] | **Format:** Zoom/in-person, casual setup
> **Your benefit:** Featured on our website + LinkedIn (great for your portfolio/brand visibility)

---

## INTERVIEW SCRIPT (Read as Casual Conversation, Not Robotic)

### SEGMENT 1: INTRODUCTION (30s)

**Interviewer:** "Hi [Name], thanks so much for taking the time. Can you tell us a bit about yourself and your property business?"

**Expected answer:** *2-3 sentences about their role, company size, number of properties*

**If they hesitate, prompt:** "How many properties do you manage? What's your role?"

---

### SEGMENT 2: THE PAIN POINT (45s)

**Interviewer:** "Before Premiso, what was your biggest headache managing properties? What system were you using?"

**Key points they should touch on:**
- ✓ Multiple systems (spreadsheets, emails, separate compliance tracker, etc.)
- ✓ Missed deadlines (gas certs, inspections, rent reminders)
- ✓ Time wasted juggling tools or manual data entry
- ✓ Stress/anxiety about compliance gaps

**If vague, prompt:** "Give me an example of a time you nearly missed something important—how did that feel?"

---

### SEGMENT 3: THE SWITCH (45s)

**Interviewer:** "What made you decide to try Premiso? And what was the first thing that surprised you?"

**Expected answer:**
- ✓ How they found out about Premiso (referral, search, etc.)
- ✓ What caught their attention (compliance focus, all-in-one, etc.)
- ✓ First impression or quick win (e.g., "The dashboard immediately showed me overdue tasks," "Setting up took 30 minutes, not a week")

**If hesitant, prompt:** "What feature or moment made you think, 'Oh, this actually works'?"

---

### SEGMENT 4: SPECIFIC IMPACT (60s) — **Most Important**

**Interviewer:** "Walk us through one specific thing that changed after you started using Premiso. How does your day/week look different now?"

**BEST ANSWERS have specifics:**
- ✓ "I used to spend 3 hours every Friday checking compliance dates. Now Premiso sends me alerts on Monday, so I'm never caught off guard."
- ✓ "We missed a gas safety cert renewal in 2024—cost us £800 + stress. With Premiso, we got the alert 30 days before and renewed it on time."
- ✓ "My tenants can now submit maintenance requests directly. Before, I was getting calls, emails, texts—all over the place. Now it's centralized, and I can assign directly to contractors."
- ✓ "The financial reporting is insane. I used to spend a week putting together landlord statements. Now I generate them in 5 minutes."

**If they're vague, push:** "Give me a number: How much time did that save you per week/month?"

---

### SEGMENT 5: RECOMMENDATION (45s)

**Interviewer:** "Would you recommend Premiso to other property managers like yourself? Why or why not?"

**Expected answer:**
- ✓ Yes, because [specific reason]
- ✓ Who it's best for (BTL landlords, letting agents, block managers, etc.)
- ✓ One thing they wish was different (honest feedback is credible)

**Example strong answer:**
> "100%. If you're managing more than 2-3 properties or worried about compliance, Premiso saves you from disaster. The only thing I'd add is better integration with my accounting software, but the team said that's coming soon."

---

### SEGMENT 6: CLOSING (15s)

**Interviewer:** "Any final thoughts for people thinking about trying Premiso?"

**Best answers:**
- "Just do it. Don't overthink it."
- "Set aside an hour to import your data, and you'll wonder why you didn't do it sooner."
- "If you're still using spreadsheets, you're leaving money on the table."

## POST-INTERVIEW PRODUCTION NOTES

### Filming Best Practices
- **Location:** Quiet background (home office OK, noisy café not OK)
- **Lighting:** Face lit, no strong shadows (natural window light ideal)
- **Audio:** Use phone mic or USB headset (built-in laptop mic = tinny)
- **Framing:** Head & shoulders, person slightly off-center (cinema style)
- **Length:** Aim for 2-3 min final edit (expect 8-10 min raw interview)

### Editing Notes
- **Cut interview into 2 versions:**
  1. **Full testimonial** (2-3 min) — Landing page, YouTube
  2. **Social clip** (30-45s) — LinkedIn, TikTok (Segment 4 + closing usually works best)
- **Add:** Premiso dashboard b-roll behind them during Segments 2-3 (show problem → solution visually)
- **Graphics:** Company name, role, location as lower third
- **Music:** Upbeat, subtle (royalty-free from YouTube Audio Library)
- **Color grade:** Bright, clean, professional

## RECORDING LOGISTICS

### Equipment Needed
- **Interviewer:** Zoom account (or in-person)
- **Camera:** Phone camera (iPhone 12+ = excellent) or webcam
- **Mic:** AirPods or basic USB headset ($20-50)
- **Backup:** Record locally + Zoom cloud backup

### Recording Checklist
- [ ] Test audio levels (no peaking)
- [ ] Test internet connection (stable WiFi)
- [ ] Ask permission: "Can I record and use this for marketing?"
- [ ] Record multiple takes if needed (e.g., Segment 4 is critical—ask twice if first answer is vague)
- [ ] Get written consent (simple email: "OK to use this on our website + social media?")

## SCRIPT TWEAKS BY CUSTOMER TYPE

### If Beta User = BTL Landlord
- Focus on: Time savings, tenant screening, rent reminders
- Quote to highlight: Time spent on admin, peace of mind

### If Beta User = Letting Agent
- Focus on: Tenant management, compliance tracking, portfolio scale
- Quote to highlight: Managing more properties with same team

### If Beta User = Block Management
- Focus on: Service charges, leaseholder communication, compliance burden
- Quote to highlight: Automation, audit trail, regulatory confidence

---

## RED FLAGS (If They Say These, Retake)

❌ "It's pretty good." (Too vague—need specifics)  
❌ "I like how it looks." (Too surface-level—we want business impact)  
❌ "It saves us time." (Needs number: how much? What task?)  
❌ "Everyone should use it." (Why? For whom? Be specific)

**Fix:** Pause, clarify with a prompt, re-record that segment.
