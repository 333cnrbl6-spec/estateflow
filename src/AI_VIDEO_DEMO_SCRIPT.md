## PRODUCTION NOTES

**Recommended Tools:**
- **HeyGen** (AI video with avatar) — fast, professional, lip-synced
- **Synthesia** (AI video) — similar quality
- **Loom** + screen recording (if you want live product walkthrough)

**Visual Style:**
- Clean, modern, minimal animations
- Use app screenshots/recordings as base
- Add subtle transitions between scenes
- Color scheme: Premiso primary blue (#1C3D8E), accent green (#7ED321), light backgrounds

**Music:**
- Upbeat, modern, not distracting
- "Tech startup" energy without being cliché
- 120-130 BPM

**Tone:**
- Conversational, not salesy
- UK English (use "about" not "round", "postcode" not "zip")
- Empathy for pain points, confidence in solution
