## SENDING STRATEGY

### Timing
- **Best day:** Tuesday–Thursday (avoid Monday chaos, Friday inboxes)
- **Best time:** 9–11 AM or 2–3 PM (working hours)

### Personalization
- Always research the recipient before sending (find them on LinkedIn, check recent articles)
- Mention something specific ("I saw your article on X, great point about Y")
- Use their first name, not "Dear Hiring Manager"

### Follow-up
- No response after 5 days? Send a light follow-up: "Just checking if this landed okay. Would love to chat when you have time."
- No response after 10 days? Move on. Not everyone is interested.

### Tracking
Keep a spreadsheet:
| Organization | Contact | Date Sent | Response | Next Step |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ARLA | Jane Smith | 16-Apr | Waiting | Follow up 21-Apr |
| NAEA | Tom Jones | 16-Apr | Interested | Schedule call |
| Xero | Sarah Lee | 16-Apr | No reply | Follow up 21-Apr |

### Success Metrics
- **Target:** 10% response rate (1 positive response per 10 emails = success)
- **Good outcome:** 1 partner per 100 outreach emails
- **Great outcome:** 2–3 partnerships per 100

## BEST PRACTICES

✅ **DO:**
- Personalize every email (not mass-send)
- Lead with their benefit, not your product features
- Offer something free/low-cost first (trial, webinar, checklist)
- Be specific about the partnership (not vague "let's talk")
- Follow up once, then let it go
- Track everything (spreadsheet or Pipedrive)

❌ **DON'T:**
- Send generic "partnership" emails (they get 50 a day)
- Lead with sales pitch ("Here's our pricing")
- Demand a call without context
- Send to wrong contact (research first)
- Follow up more than twice (annoying)
- Send at 5 PM on Friday (will be forgotten by Monday)
