# Premiso Assistant - Helper Bot Quick Start

## Overview
The Premiso Assistant is an AI-powered helper bot available throughout the app. It provides contextual support, answers questions, and guides users through common tasks.

## Accessing the Helper Bot

1. **Look for the message icon** in the bottom-right corner of the screen
2. **Click to open** the chat interface
3. **Ask any question** - the bot understands your current location in the app and provides contextual help

## What the Helper Bot Can Do

### Answer Questions
- "What is a tenancy agreement?"
- "How do I track maintenance requests?"
- "What are the compliance requirements?"
- "How do I integrate with Xero?"

### Guide You Through Tasks
- "How do I create a new property?"
- "What steps do I need to set up a tenancy?"
- "How do I schedule maintenance?"
- "What's the process for collecting rent?"

### Provide Compliance Advice
- "What certificates do I need to maintain?"
- "When are certificates due for renewal?"
- "What's the Right to Rent process?"
- "What are my HRRB responsibilities?"

### Suggest Features
- "What features would help me manage vacancies?"
- "How can I automate tenant communications?"
- "What integrations are available?"

## Page-Specific Help

The bot is context-aware. When you visit different pages, it understands:
- **Dashboard** - Executive overview and key metrics
- **Properties** - Portfolio and unit management
- **Tenants** - Tenant lifecycle and communications
- **Maintenance** - Work order and contractor management
- **Compliance** - Certificate tracking and alerts
- **Financials** - Rent, expenses, and reporting
- **Sales** - Leads, listings, and transactions
- **Block Management** - Service charges and leaseholders

## Tips for Best Results

1. **Be specific** - "How do I create a maintenance request for a plumbing leak?" works better than "maintenance"
2. **Ask step-by-step** - "What do I do first?" or "What's the next step?"
3. **Use suggested actions** - Click on suggested actions shown in responses
4. **Ask follow-ups** - You can ask clarifying questions after the initial response

## Keyboard Shortcuts

- **Focus on bot** - Press `?` from anywhere to focus the helper bot
- **Send message** - Press `Enter` after typing
- **Close bot** - Click the X button or press `Escape`

## Integrations Supported

The bot can answer questions about:
- Xero, Sage, QuickBooks (accounting)
- Google Calendar, Drive (productivity)
- Stripe (payments)
- DocuSign (e-signatures)
- Slack (communications)

## Tips for Getting Unstuck

1. **Describe what you're trying to do** - "I need to track rent payments for property X"
2. **Mention error messages** - Include exact error text if applicable
3. **Ask for alternatives** - "Is there another way to...?"
4. **Request step-by-step** - "Can you break that down into steps?"
