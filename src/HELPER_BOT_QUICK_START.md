# Helper Bot - Quick Start Guide

## What is the Helper Bot?

The Helper Bot is your AI-powered assistant for navigating Premiso. It's always available as a floating house icon in the bottom-right corner of your screen.

## Getting Started

### 1. Open the Bot
Click the **blue house icon** 🏠 in the bottom-right corner

### 2. See Suggestions
The bot shows **module-specific suggestions** based on where you are in the app

### 3. Ask Questions
Type any question about Premiso and get instant, intelligent answers

### 4. Take Action
Click **suggested buttons** to navigate directly to the right place

## Features

### 💡 Smart Suggestions
When you're in a module, the bot suggests common actions:
- **Dashboard**: "View portfolio performance", "Check compliance alerts"
- **Sales**: "Create sales listing", "Generate property valuation"
- **Financials**: "Process rent payments", "Run financial reports"

### 🎯 Quick Actions
Always-available shortcuts to common tasks:
- Add a new property
- Create maintenance request
- Upload compliance certificate
- Process rent payment

### 🤖 AI Assistant
Ask anything about Premiso:
- "How do I add a tenant?"
- "Where can I view financial reports?"
- "How do I generate a property valuation?"
- "What is the out of hours service?"

### 📍 Context-Aware
The bot knows which page you're on and provides relevant help!

## Examples
